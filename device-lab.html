Measure the phone before designing its model.

This page checks browser-visible device capabilities and runs a short graphics workload on your Galaxy A26. It does not generate AI video, download a model, or send device data anywhere. The benchmark is a planning test—not a prediction of neural-network speed.

Graphics workload test

Runs a small WebGL shader workload for up to 8 seconds, then reports measured frame rate and canvas dimensions. Keep the phone cool and close other heavy apps first. Stop if the device becomes hot.

What this test means

Important: A graphics benchmark does not prove a video model will fit or run. Neural video generation needs model weights, a compatible inference runtime, substantial memory, and thermal testing. This prototype deliberately does not pretend a procedural animation is AI.

The responsible next step is to record this device's actual browser benchmark, then build a native Android inference spike with an explicitly licensed tiny model. Only after running real model weights on this phone can we report whether offline generation works.
